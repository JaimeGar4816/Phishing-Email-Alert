# Privacy Policy

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data. The extension operates entirely locally within your browser and only analyzes emails to detect phishing attempts. All flagged links and threats are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API and are never sent to external servers.

## Data Storage
- Phishing detection results are stored locally in your browser only
- No data is sent to Anthropic, Google, or any third-party servers
- Clearing your browser data will clear all stored results

## Permissions
The extension only requests the storage permission to keep detection results locally available in the popup.
